 TWITTPAY - PERFECT SMM PANEL MODULE
 version 1.1.0
================================================================================

READ THIS FIRST: the three PHP files in this zip are BLOCKS TO PASTE, not files
to upload. Each one starts in the middle of an if/elseif chain that already
exists in your panel. Uploading them on top of your own files will break your
panel.


WHAT GOES WHERE
---------------

1. payment_methods.sql
   Run it once in phpMyAdmin on your panel database. It adds the payment method
   as id 71.

2. admin/controller/ajax_data.php
   Open your panel's own admin/controller/ajax_data.php, find the chain of
   "elseif (($action == 'edit_paymentmethod') && ($_POST['id'] == '...'))"
   blocks, and paste the block from this file in among them.

   This is the admin form: Method name, Visibility, Visible name, Minimum,
   address you will need in step 5.

3. app/controller/addfunds.php
   Two pieces:
     - the two small "if (isset($_GET[...]))" blocks go near the top of your
       panel's own app/controller/addfunds.php
     - the "elseif ($method_id == 71)" block goes into the chain of payment
       methods further down the same file

4. app/controller/payment.php
   Paste the "if ($method_name == 'twittpay')" block into your panel's own
   app/controller/payment.php, alongside the other payment method blocks.


THEN CONFIGURE IT
-----------------

5. In your panel: Admin -> Settings -> Payment methods -> TwittPay -> edit.

     Brand Key         the key from your TwittPay brand
     Exchange Rate   how many BDT one unit of your panel currency is worth.
                     Leave it at 100 if your panel is in USD and 1 USD = 100 BDT.
                     If your panel is already in BDT, set it to 1.

   The same screen shows the API callback address, which looks like this:

       https://yourpanel.com/payment/twittpay

6. Give that callback address, plus your panel's own domain, to whoever runs the
   gateway so they can add it to your brand's allowed domains. The gateway
   refuses a payment whose callback points at a domain it does not know.


WHY 1.1.0 EXISTS
----------------

Version 1.0 could not work at all. It sent the Brand Key under a header named
"twittpay-API-KEY", and the gateway reads "API-KEY". A key under a name
nobody reads is the same as no key, so every request was refused, no payment
page was ever produced, and no balance was ever credited.

The rest of 1.1.0 is about pending payments. When a customer pays but the SMS
has not arrived yet, the gateway parks the payment and the merchant approves it
by hand, sometimes hours later. By then the customer's browser is long gone.
The gateway therefore calls your panel twice:

      first call    PENDING     nothing is credited yet
      second call   COMPLETED   after the merchant approves - credit the balance
      or            FAILED      if the merchant rejects it

Version 1.0 treated the first call as a failure, and its "mark as failed" SQL
had commas where it needed AND, so it could not even do that. Version 1.1.0
leaves a pending payment exactly as it is and waits for the second call.

Full details are in the comment at the top of each PHP file.


CHECKING IT
-----------

Add funds with TwittPay. You should reach the gateway's payment page. If
you get an error instead of a payment page, the message on screen is the
panel's domain not being on the brand's allowed list.

To test the pending path: pay, then give a transaction id with no SMS behind it,
then CLOSE THE TAB. Ask the merchant to approve it in the gateway panel. The
balance should appear in your panel on its own, with no visit from you.
